# Audit rule catalog

Generated by `python3 scripts/audit.py --list-rules`. Do not edit by hand.

Scoring: start at 100, subtract CRITICAL 25, HIGH 10, MEDIUM 4, LOW 1, INFO 0. Grade A>=90, B>=80, C>=70, D>=60, else F. Any CRITICAL caps the grade at D, and any HIGH caps it at B.

Suppress inline with `# vibe-ship: ignore VS-XXXX <reason>` (same or next line, or anywhere in the final stage for stage-level Dockerfile rules) or `# vibe-ship: ignore-file VS-XXXX`.
